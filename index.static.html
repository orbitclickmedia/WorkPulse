FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the AI copilot work? ↓
The AI Copilot has full context of your organization's standup data, blocker history, and team metrics. You can ask natural language questions like "Who missed updates this week?" or "Show recurring blockers" and get intelligent, data-driven responses powered by Claude AI.
Is our data isolated from other organizations? ↓
Yes. WorkPulse uses a strict multi-tenant architecture with Firestore security rules ensuring complete organization isolation. Your data is never shared with or visible to other organizations.
What integrations are available? ↓
WorkPulse integrates with Slack, Microsoft Teams, Google Calendar, Jira, Linear, and GitHub. Enterprise plans also support custom webhooks and API access.
Can I export reports? ↓
Absolutely. WorkPulse supports PDF, XLSX, and CSV exports for all reports — including branded executive summaries with charts, AI-generated narratives, and department comparisons. You can also schedule automatic report delivery.
